Get your truck on the map

Free for Southern Colorado food trucks. No listing fee and no commission. We confirm details with you and get your approval before the listing appears.

Sign up in one message

Open the Where We Eatin' listing desk and send: truck name / what you sell / customer phone / usual location / service days or hours.

Message the Telegram listing desk →

The desk is operated by a person; it is not an instant self-serve form.

What happens next

  1. Your message is recorded as a vendor lead for follow-up.
  2. The operator confirms the details with you and shares the listing text for approval.
  3. Only after approval is the listing published.

Already listed?

Find your truck’s page and message the listing desk to correct details or ask about optional paid plans. Free listings remain free. Ratings and the HIGHLY RECOMMENDED badge are earned from genuine customer reviews, never sold.

What customers see

Your truck’s listed specialty, customer phone, usual area, and any location check-in you choose to share. A check-in is temporary; it is not continuous GPS tracking. Menu prices remain marked unconfirmed until you confirm them.
