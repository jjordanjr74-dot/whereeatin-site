Get your truck listed

Free for Southern Colorado food trucks. No commission, no listing fee, and you approve the listing before a customer ever sees it.

How to sign up: message our listing desk on Telegram and answer five questions.

Message the listing desk →

What we need from you

  • Truck name
  • What you cook
  • The number customers should call
  • Where you usually park
  • Your weekly hours
  • One photo, if you have it handy

What you get

  • A listing on the radar, with the location you actually park at
  • Customers contact you directly — we take no cut of your sales and no delivery markup
  • HIGHLY RECOMMENDED is earned from real reviews, never sold
  • You can be removed at any time, with no notice period

How it works after you message us

  1. Your message lands with the person who runs the radar (not an automated queue).
  2. We confirm the details with you and show you the listing text before it goes live.
  3. It appears on the radar and in the public truck list on the home page.
