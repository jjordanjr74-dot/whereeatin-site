Privacy Policy

Where We Eatin'? — the live food-truck radar for Pueblo and Colorado Springs. Last updated: 2026-09-19.

Short version: we collect only what a truck listing, a reservation enquiry or a review needs. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising trackers, and card details never touch our servers.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhyWhere it lives
Vendor listing details (business name, category, city, current location, specials, phone) To show the truck on the radar and let customers contact it directly Vendor table on the operator's own machine
Order and enquiry details (customer name, phone, items, event type, guest count, city, budget) To pass a catering or order request to the vendor that has to fulfil it Orders / event-planning tables
Review content (stars, recommended flag, comment, first name, last initial) To show other customers how a truck has performed. Full surnames are deliberately not asked for Reviews table
A one-way hash of the reviewer's IP address Only to slow down fake or duplicated reviews. The original address is not stored and cannot be recovered from the hash Reviews table
Location you type or share when you press “use my location” To sort trucks by distance. Only the search text and resolved coordinates are cached, to avoid re-asking the geocoder Geocode cache
Payment session reference To reconcile a completed vendor subscription or booking fee Handled by Stripe; we store only the session id, never a card number or CVC

Who else is involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and sees standard web-server logs (IP, user agent).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim resolves the location text you type into coordinates.
  • Stripe processes payments when a vendor pays. Their privacy policy governs card data.
  • Telegram: operational notices (for example, a nightly batch summary) go to the operator's own Telegram account, not to a marketing list.

How long we keep it

  • Vendor listings: as long as the truck is listed, and removed on request.
  • Orders and enquiries: kept until the event is served and any dispute window passes.
  • Reviews: kept while the listing is live; removed with the listing.
  • Geocode cache: transient, overwritten as new searches arrive.

Your choices

  • Ask us to correct or delete a listing, a review, or an enquiry — including one you submitted as a customer.
  • Vendors can ask to be removed from the radar at any time; the listing is deleted, not hidden.
  • We do not send marketing messages to customers. Vendor outreach is one-to-one and stops when asked.

Children

This service is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
