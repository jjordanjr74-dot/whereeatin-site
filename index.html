The honest answers

Questions people actually ask.

Do you take a cut of what I spend at the truck?

No. There is no ordering, no delivery and no card processing here — you pay the truck directly, which is why everything costs what the truck charges.

How do you know where a truck is right now?

Only when the truck checks in with us, or confirms it is serving. A check-in is labelled live and expires after four hours. Every other truck shows its usual area, which means exactly that: their normal patch, not a live position. We don't pretend otherwise.

Does it cost a truck anything to be listed?

No. Listing is free forever and includes the radar pin, menu, phone number and reviews. Optional paid plans are Professional at $15/month and Premier at $35/month; neither affects ratings.

Can a truck buy the HIGHLY RECOMMENDED badge?

No. It requires 3+ reviews, a 4.5+ average and no review below 3 stars in the last 90 days. It recalculates on every review, so it can be lost as well as earned.

Who are you?

An independent local directory started in Pueblo, not a franchise, not a delivery app. If we can't prove something on this site with a real number, we say so — including that we have 0 reviews and 31 trucks so far.

I'm a truck and I want in. What do I send?

Message @Sheba_telegram_bot and send your truck name, what you sell, your phone, and your usual area — free to list, no commission. Check the For trucks page for the full details.
