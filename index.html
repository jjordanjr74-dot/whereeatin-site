Ratings, comments, one badge that matters

Reviews humans write. A badge trucks earn.

Zero reviews so far — and that number is the honest one. A review is one message: “5 Big Papa's Grill — best brisket in Pueblo”.

Nothing here is seeded, bought, or written by us — which is why this page now says zero. The first review comes from a real customer message.

Earned, never sold: HIGHLY RECOMMENDED needs at least 3 reviews, an average of 4.5+ and no review below 3 stars in the last 90 days. It is recalculated on every review, so it can be lost. One review per truck per 12 hours per person; only a first name and last initial are stored.

The honest answers

Questions people actually ask.

Do you take a cut of what I spend at the truck?

No. There is no ordering, no delivery and no card processing here — you pay the truck directly, which is why everything costs what the truck charges.

How do you know where a truck is right now?

Vendors send their spot to our listing desk and the pin moves within minutes; those trucks are labelled “vendor reported”. Any other truck is marked “usual spot”, which means exactly that — their normal patch, not a live position. We don't pretend otherwise.

Does it cost a truck anything to be listed?

No. Listing is free forever and includes the radar pin, menu, phone number and reviews. The only paid option is Featured at $35/month, and it has no effect on ratings.

Can a truck buy the HIGHLY RECOMMENDED badge?

No. It requires 3+ reviews, a 4.5+ average and no review below 3 stars in the last 90 days. It recalculates on every review, so it can be lost as well as earned.

Who are you?

An independent local directory started in Pueblo, not a franchise, not a delivery app. If we can't prove something on this site with a real number, we say so — including that we have 0 reviews and 9 trucks so far.

I'm a truck and I want in. What do I send?

One message: truck name, what you cook, phone number, where you usually park and which days. We send back a page to approve before anything appears publicly.
